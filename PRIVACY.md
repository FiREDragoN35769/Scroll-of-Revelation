# ScrolL of RevelatioN Privacy Policy (Draft)
Last updated: 2026-10-25

ScrolL of RevelatioN processes screenshots you explicitly select on your Android device. It creates PDF files locally and saves them only to the location you choose through Android's system document picker.

The current version does not request internet access, send screenshots to a server, collect analytics, display ads, or require an account. It does not automatically capture other apps. Screenshots may contain sensitive information, so review your files before sharing them with anyone.

The app accesses only images selected through the Android system picker. It does not request broad storage access.

For Google Play publication, this policy needs a public hosted URL and a developer contact address. This draft does not claim that the app has been approved or published.
